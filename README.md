## Cybersecurity news I read recently

**Canvas (Instructure) data breach**

Instructure is the company that makes Canvas, which many colleges and schools use. A hacking group called ShinyHunters said they broke into their systems and stole a huge amount of data (they claim over 3.65TB). Because Canvas is used by thousands of institutions, the personal info of a lot of students and staff was affected.

**What I learned from this:** The attack was on a third-party company, not on the colleges directly. So even if your college is secure, you can still be affected if a company you depend on gets hacked.
